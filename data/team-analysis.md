## A note on your league's scoring

Your league awards **2 points for any 40+ yard run or reception**, plus bonuses at 100 and 125 rushing yards. That is *not* the Yahoo default, and it quietly rewards explosive players over high-volume possession types. Every projection in the hub already accounts for it, because they were pulled directly from your league rather than a generic ranking site — which is why some numbers here won't match FantasyPros or ESPN.
